Three directions

Each file is complete and standalone. Open one, then copy it over index.html and delete the rest.

  • A Drifting dot grid Monospace, left-aligned, muted teal. Two dot grids drifting against each other. The most restrained of the three.
  • B Animated gradient mesh Geometric sans, centred, indigo and teal. Slow-moving soft blobs behind a scrim. The softest and largest-scale.
  • C Grain and scanline Monospace uppercase, amber on warm black. Static film grain plus one slow scanline sweep. The most overtly terminal.

This chooser is temporary and marked noindex. All three variants use system fonts only, make zero network requests, and freeze their background under prefers-reduced-motion.
